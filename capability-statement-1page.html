What We Do

GotHawk Solutions LLC delivers AI governance and compliance technology to federal contractors, DoD program offices, and government agencies. Our product, PromptFrame, is a deterministic, air-gapped evidence engine that scores an organization's AI systems against federal AI mandates and auto-generates theAI-governance evidence package for ATO preparation — including machine-readable OSCAL that feeds any authorization platform (eMASS, Xacta, RegScale). No LLM in the scoring path — same input always produces the same result. Fully self-hosted; no client data ever leaves the environment.

Core Capabilities
  • AI Governance Scoring — deterministic 10-dimension assessment of AI system prompts; no LLM, C3PAO and 3PAO defensible; verbatim regulatory citations from primary sources.
  • ATO Evidence Generation — SSP narratives, POA&M, NIST 800-53 crosswalk, GSAR checklist, SPRS export, remediation report, executive summary; SHA-256 integrity-protected, machine-readable OSCAL (SSP + POA&M).
  • Shadow AI & Foreign AI Detection — scans for installed AI tools, extensions, and network contacts; flags foreign-origin AI endpoints per GSAR 552.239-7001 (proposed).
  • AI Governance Advisory & Teaming — fixed-scope readiness assessments; available as subcontractor under prime AI-modernization and DoD agentic-AI efforts. We team with an ISSO/3PAO partner for ATO execution.
